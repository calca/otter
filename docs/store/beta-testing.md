## A short guide for testers

Thanks for trying Calm Otter! It's a calm, offline app: nothing you do in
it leaves your phone.

**Setting up (2 minutes)**

1. Open the app and follow the three steps. The password is meant for
   someone you trust; for testing, choose one you'll remember.
2. Tap the otter: the app asks for two permissions, Accessibility and Do
   Not Disturb. They're what make the pause work.

**What we'd love you to try**

- **A pause on your own**: pick a duration, tap the otter, then try to open
  another app. End it early with the password, or wait for it to finish
  and answer "How was it?".
- **One tap** (phones with NFC, both with the *same* version of the app):
  one person taps "One tap", then hold the phones back to back. Try it also
  with the other phone's app closed.
- **Time together** without NFC: a QR or a code, or the Bluetooth lobby.
- **Scheduled pauses**: Settings → The pause → Scheduled pauses. Allow
  "Alarms & reminders" when asked, set one in a few minutes, lock the phone
  and leave it.
- **The widget** on your home screen, and the "Calm Otter" tile in Quick
  Settings.
- **A phone call during a pause**: it should always get through.

**Telling us what happened**

Settings → About → **Send feedback** opens a short form on GitHub with the
app version and your phone already filled in (you need a GitHub account).
The most useful reports say what you expected, what happened instead, and
roughly when.
